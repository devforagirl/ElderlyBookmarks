Privacy Policy

At Elderly Friendly Bookmarks, we believe that privacy is a fundamental right, especially for the vulnerable users we serve.

"The developer has disclosed that it will not collect or use your data. To learn more, see the developer’s privacy policy."

Our Data Commitments

This developer declares that your data is:

  • Not being sold to third parties, outside of the approved use cases.
  • Not being used or transferred for purposes that are unrelated to the item's core functionality.
  • Not being used or transferred to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Technical Implementation

To ensure these commitments are met, the extension is built as a local-only application. It interacts directly with the Chrome Bookmarks API on your device. No data is sent to any external server, and no tracking scripts or telemetry are included in the code.
