Core Privacy Commitment

Elderly Friendly Bookmarks is designed with a "Privacy First" philosophy. Our commitment to your privacy includes:

  • Local Data Only: Your bookmarks are stored and managed entirely within your browser. We do not use any external servers to store your data.
  • No Tracking: We do not use Google Analytics, telemetry, or any form of tracking scripts.
  • Minimum Permissions: We only request the minimum permissions (bookmarks, favicon, tabs) required to provide the core functionality of the extension.
  • Open Source: Our code is 100% open-source and available for public audit.
